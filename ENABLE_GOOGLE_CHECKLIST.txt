╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║          GOOGLE SIGN-IN SETUP CHECKLIST                      ║
║          Follow these steps in order                          ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

□ Step 1: Open Firebase Console
  → Go to: https://console.firebase.google.com/
  → Select your project

□ Step 2: Navigate to Authentication
  → Click "Authentication" in left sidebar
  → Click "Sign-in method" tab

□ Step 3: Enable Google Provider
  → Find "Google" in the list
  → Click on it
  → Toggle "Enable" to ON (should turn green)
  → Select support email from dropdown
  → Click "Save" button

□ Step 4: Check Authorized Domains
  → Click "Settings" tab (still in Authentication)
  → Scroll to "Authorized domains" section
  → Verify "localhost" is in the list
  → If not, click "Add domain" and add "localhost"

□ Step 5: Test
  → Go back to your app (http://localhost:8000)
  → Refresh the page (F5)
  → Click "🔐 Login / Sign Up"
  → Click "Continue with Google"
  → Should open Google account picker popup

╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║                    VERIFICATION                               ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

When Google Sign-In is properly enabled, you should see:

✓ In Firebase Console → Authentication → Sign-in method:
  - Google: Enabled (green toggle)
  - Status: "Enabled"
  - Support email: [your email]

✓ In Firebase Console → Authentication → Settings:
  - Authorized domains includes: localhost

✓ In your app:
  - Google popup opens
  - Can select Google account
  - Redirects back to app
  - Email appears in header
  - No error messages

╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║                  TROUBLESHOOTING                              ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

If still getting error:

1. Check browser console (F12 → Console tab)
   Look for: "Error code: auth/operation-not-allowed"
   
2. Verify Firebase config in firebase-config.js
   - No "YOUR_" placeholders
   - authDomain matches your project
   
3. Try clearing browser cache:
   - Ctrl + Shift + Delete
   - Clear cached images and files
   - Close and reopen browser

4. Try Email/Password instead:
   - Click "Sign Up" tab
   - Create account with email
   - This should work without Google

╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║              ALTERNATIVE: SKIP GOOGLE SIGN-IN                 ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

You can use the app WITHOUT Google Sign-In:

1. Click "🔐 Login / Sign Up" button
2. Click "Sign Up" tab
3. Enter email and password (min 6 characters)
4. Click "Sign Up"
5. Done! You're logged in

Email/Password is already enabled and ready to use.
